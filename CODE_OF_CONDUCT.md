# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone contributing to or using the Minecraft Network project, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Standards

- **Professionalism and Respect:** Treat all collaborators, maintainers, and users with courtesy and constructive intent.
- **Constructive Collaboration:** Focus on what is best for the project and community. Critique ideas and code constructively, not individuals.
- **Graceful Acceptance of Feedback:** Welcome constructive technical feedback and architectural critique objectively.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Offensive comments, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks.
- Harassment in public or private communications.
- Publishing private personal information without explicit consent.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. If you witness or experience unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers will handle reports promptly and confidentially.
